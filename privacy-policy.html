Privacy Policy

I-Ching Consultation App

Effective date: 13 November 2026

1. Introduction

I-Ching Consultation App is a Flutter-based application designed for professional I-Ching consultants. It helps consultants record consultation details, manually enter coin-throw results, document interpretations and generate client-ready PDF reports.

The app is designed to operate offline. It does not require user registration, an online account or a developer-operated server.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled by the application, how it is stored, and how users can manage or delete it.

2. Information Handled by the App

The application handles only one category of information.

A. Client Consultation Information

During a consultation, the consultant may enter:

  • Client name
  • Date of birth
  • Time of birth
  • Place of birth
  • Consultation questions
  • Question categories
  • Coin-throw results and generated hexagram details
  • Consultant-entered interpretations and comments
  • Consultation dates and timestamps

This information is maintained in temporary application memory during the active consultation session.

Client consultation information is not intentionally saved to a permanent database or transmitted to the developer.

When the consultation session is ended and the application's session data is cleared, the temporary consultation information is removed. Information held only in memory may also be lost if the application process terminates.

3. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the application is used exclusively to provide its intended functionality.

This includes:

  • Identifying the client in a consultation.
  • Recording consultation questions and their associated details.
  • Generating hexagram results from manually entered coin-throw data.
  • Recording consultant-entered interpretations.
  • Compiling consultation information into an A5 PDF report.

The application does not automatically generate interpretations or transmit consultation information to an external analysis service.

4. Data Storage and Security

The application is designed to function offline.

  • Client consultation information is maintained in temporary application memory.
  • No developer-operated cloud storage or remote database is used.
  • The application does not implement a separate cloud backup or synchronisation service for this information.

Since consultation information is maintained temporarily in application memory rather than persistently stored by the app, it is not retained as a local application database. Users should nevertheless secure their devices with appropriate screen locks and device security.

The developer does not receive or have routine access to information entered into the application.

5. PDF Reports and Sharing

The application can generate A5 PDF reports containing client details, consultation questions, hexagram information and consultant-entered interpretations.

PDF generation takes place locally on the device.

Users may choose to share or export the generated report using the device's available printing and sharing functionality.

Once a report is shared or saved to another application, its storage, security, retention and subsequent use are governed by the policies and practices of the selected destination or recipient.

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have appropriate permission to share consultation reports and personal information.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

The application does not operate a backend server and does not transmit consultation information to the developer.

The application does not include:

  • Advertising SDKs
  • Analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Crash-reporting services
  • Cloud-based consultation storage
  • User account or authentication services

The application does not sell, rent or share consultation information with the developer or advertising networks.

When users voluntarily export or share PDF reports, information may be handled by the external applications or services they select.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Client Consultation Information

Consultation information is held temporarily during the active application session. It is cleared when the session is explicitly ended through the application's Exit functionality. It may also be lost when the application process terminates.

Users should export any report they wish to retain before ending the consultation session.

Exported PDF Reports

PDF reports saved or shared outside the application are not automatically deleted when the application session ends. Users must manage those copies separately.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is designed as a professional consultation tool for adult practitioners. It is not specifically designed for children.

The developer does not knowingly collect children's information through an online service. Any client information entered into a consultation is handled locally as described in this policy.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, data handling practices or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be made available at the published Privacy Policy URL.

Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
